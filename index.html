Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusX handles information when you use the FocusX Android application, a productivity-focused launcher and digital wellbeing companion.

Privacy-first design: FocusX is designed to process supported AI requests and usage-monitoring information on your device where possible. Android permissions are used to provide the features you enable.

1. Information and permissions

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, FocusX may access or process the following information on your device:

  • App usage information: foreground app usage and timing information, used to display screen-time insights and support distraction-prevention features.
  • Accessibility service events: if enabled, the FocusX accessibility service may observe app/window transitions needed to detect distracting app use and show reminders. It is not intended to collect passwords or message contents.
  • Goals, reminders, alarms, and focus sessions: details you enter, such as goal text, reminder text, and scheduled times, to provide those functions.
  • Local AI prompts: text you provide to the built-in local language model may be processed on your device to generate a response.
  • Installed app information: app names and package identifiers may be accessed to display your app list and launch apps.
  • Optional permissions: notification, phone-state, camera, notification-policy, and other Android permissions may be requested where needed by a feature you choose to use.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the launcher, calculate usage summaries, run focus sessions, trigger alarms and reminders, provide goal-related prompts, and support the AI assistant. FocusX does not sell personal information.

3. On-device processing and network services

FocusX is designed to keep supported usage monitoring, reminders, goals, and local AI processing on your device. Some operations may require an internet connection, including downloading the AI model, checking or managing purchases through Google Play, or accessing an online service you explicitly use. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Do not enter highly sensitive information into the AI assistant unless you are comfortable doing so. If a future version adds cloud AI, analytics, advertising, or other data-sharing services, this policy should be updated before that version is released.

4. Storage and retention

Feature settings and related information may be stored locally so FocusX can remember preferences and maintain reminders or usage summaries. Local information generally remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android and backup behavior. Google Play may separately retain purchase records.

5. Sharing

FocusX does not intentionally sell or share locally processed app-usage history, goals, or reminder text with the developer. Information may be handled by platform providers when needed to deliver a feature—for example, Google Play for billing—or where disclosure is required by law. Review those providers' privacy policies for details.

6. Your choices and controls

You can control optional permissions through Android Settings, disable features in the app where available, and clear FocusX's app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

7. Children's privacy

FocusX is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server. If you believe information has been provided to the developer in error, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We aim to minimize data collection and use Android's platform security and permission controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FocusX features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published here with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FocusX developer at supportfocusx@gmail.com.
